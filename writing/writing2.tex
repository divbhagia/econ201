\section{Your Essay}

Write an essay of \textbf{no more than 400 words}, entered directly as text on Canvas, that covers the following, \textbf{in this order}:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item What did you observe, where, and when, and why is it puzzling?
  \item What is your question? State it in one sentence.
  \item What is the answer? Name the course idea, explain it in plain words, and apply it to what you observed step by step. A partial answer is fine if you say what it leaves unexplained.
\end{enumerate}

The first two items are the same as in your proposal, so you can reuse what you wrote there. Write for a reader with no economics background, using plain language rather than graphs or equations, and explain each economics term the first time you use it.
